# Meridian voice

Meridian is data infrastructure for streaming pipelines. Our customers are
platform and data engineers, and the people who approve their invoices.

How we write:

- Plain, specific, engineer to engineer. Numbers carry the argument;
  adjectives do not. "p99 stayed under 40ms through the migration" is our
  kind of sentence. "Blazingly fast" is not; neither is "seamless",
  "powerful", "robust", or "supercharge".
- We state limits next to claims. If a benchmark was run on one region with
  a warm cache, the sentence that cites it says so.
- Sentence case everywhere: headings, buttons, labels. No exclamation
  points. No emoji in product or customer-facing documents.
- We say "you" to the reader and "we" for ourselves. We never write "the
  user".
- Dates are absolute (Mar 12, 2026), never "recently" or "last month".

Things reviewers keep deleting from drafts, so do not write them: "in
today's fast-paced data landscape", any sentence starting with
"Leverage", rhetorical questions as section openers.
